Privacy

Rhythm Dance & Wellness check-in · updated November 2026

This is the studio's check-in: you scan or tap the sign by the door, and the studio's attendance records fill themselves in. It collects only what that needs.

What the studio keeps

  • Your name and email, the first time you check in.
  • Your check-ins: which class, when, and the class price at the time.
  • Payments you tell it about ("Yes, I paid" after Venmo), and the packages, passes and monthly plans the studio records for you.
  • Whether you joined or hid the studio's group chat, and whether you saved a passkey. The passkey itself stays on your phone; the studio never receives it.
  • Notes the studio adds, such as a phone number or Venmo name if you give them one.
  • If you buy a class or plan on the studio's website, the purchase is matched to you by the email you check in with.

Staff who sign in with Google

The studio's owner and teachers can sign in to the studio page with Google. Google tells the studio only your email address and name, so it can check you're on its staff list. The studio never sees your password and has no access to your Google account, email, contacts or files. Your phone then keeps a sign-in key so you don't have to sign in every time; it stops working when you sign out, after 90 days unused, or when the studio takes you off its staff list.

Where it's kept, and who sees it

  • In a Google Sheet run for the studio, with nightly backup copies in the same Google Drive. The studio owner can see it; teachers see who is checked in to today's classes, but not emails, payments or what anyone owes.
  • On your own phone: your name and email (so the next check-in is one tap), and for staff the sign-in key. Clearing the site's data or tapping "Not you?" removes them.
  • This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and the records are handled by Google Apps Script, so GitHub and Google process the requests, as any web host would.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no cookies.
  • Your information is never sold or shared for marketing. It's used only to run the studio's attendance and class payments.
  • Venmo opens only when you tap Pay; this site doesn't see your Venmo account.

Your choices

Ask the studio, in person or through rhythmdw.com, to see, correct or delete what it holds about you. Attendance and payment records may be kept as long as the studio needs them for its accounts.
